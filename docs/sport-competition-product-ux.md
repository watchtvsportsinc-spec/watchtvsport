# Sport and competition product UX

Sport hubs are navigation-first rather than oversized fixture lists. Competition cards rank locally by favorite, live, today, then next scheduled event and expose confirmed TV availability.

Competition pages prioritize the next event and where-to-watch signals, then provide Upcoming / Finished / All schedule filters, participants and an About section. Monetization slots are placeholders only and do not render third-party advertising yet.

Search supports curated competition aliases and minor typo tolerance. A future traffic/popularity ranking can be layered after favorite/live/today signals when a reliable analytics signal is available.
